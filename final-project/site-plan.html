Site Name: Find-A-Book

This name represents what we hope for our page visitors, that they find a book to read.
Optional domain availability: find_a_book.com

Site Purpose:

The purpose of this site is to connect readers to books that they would be interested in reading and give them the opportunity to give a new book a chance.

Scenarios:

  • What books are popular right now?
  • Would a give a random new book a chance?
  • Do I want to know what other people are currently reading?
  • Do I want to get a monthly newsletter with book recommendations?

Color Scheme:

  • Primary color: #1E2D24, will be used for the header and footer background color.
  • Secondary color: #1F0D03, will be used for the navigation wayfinding.
  • Accent color 1: #AEB7B3, will be used used for the background of the page.
  • Accent color 2: #7E6551, will be used for the background of book cards, form, and the modal background.
  • Font color: white and black.

Typography:

Font Family: Felipa, will be used everywhere.
